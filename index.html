About

How I got here.

I'm Saroon. 17. Currently in school in India. Self-taught. Obsessed with making things.

Started the usual way — messing around with HTML, breaking things, figuring out why they broke, fixing them, breaking them again. Honestly? I still do that. Just with more confidence now.

I care about how things feel. Not just how they look. A button that doesn't feel clickable? That bothers me. A website that takes 3 seconds to load? That makes me sad.

When I'm not coding, I'm gaming. And I don't just play — I study games. UI, UX, game feel, player psychology. It's all the same thing. Make it satisfying.

I'm still early in this journey. I don't know everything. But I'm hungry. I want to build things that matter. Things people actually use. That's the goal.
